About LearnHub

We help people learn the skills that matter

LearnHub connects curious learners with experienced instructors. Our goal is simple: make quality education accessible, measurable and enjoyable.

Our mission

Learning that fits your life

Learners choose what to study, follow structured lessons at their own pace and see their progress at every step. Instructors get simple tools to publish courses, follow their students and grade their work.

LearnHub was created as an academic project to show how a modern web platform can be built with Angular, FastAPI and PostgreSQL — cleanly, securely and with accessibility in mind.
